## What this result establishes and does not

A fresh annotator can apply the supplied role distinction coherently to the
authored examples. The strongest illustrative contrast is pair 01: equivalent
rendering paths versus a change in repair instructions. The fallback pair also
supports the principle that short syntax does not establish mechanical role.

The contracts deliberately state input meanings and consequences clearly.
Agreement may depend heavily on that prose. It does not show that a classifier
can recover the same distinctions from raw historical code, infer missing human
intent, or detect them in a real repository at scale. The authored full contexts
change code names, types, contracts and effects together; the next study cannot
isolate which component caused a provider response. The withheld states also
explicitly announce missing evidence, which can make abstention easier than
detecting a subtle real-world context gap.

Four pairs, one model annotator and a nonblind integrator review remain limited
development evidence. No statistical provider comparison or threshold selection
is justified. Keep the forthcoming provider results separate from reference
agreement, preserve failed criteria, and use new natural-code families with human
adjudication for later confirmation.
